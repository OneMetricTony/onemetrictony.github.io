About the Organizer

Meet the person leading sponsorship for the team's FIRA RoboWorld Cup 2026 effort in Markham, Ontario.

Organizer

Physicist · Sponsorship Lead

Tony Chu

Tony Chu is a physicist and the sponsorship lead for the FIRA RoboWorld Cup 2026 effort in Markham, Canada. Trained at the graduate level in theoretical & mathematical physics at the University of Guelph and the University of Waterloo — with a Bachelor's from York University — his research listens in on gravitational waves, studying the collisions of neutron stars and the vibrations of black holes. In his own words, he studies the “shape of space” and the things that make it shake.

Alongside physics he works as a software & automation engineer (Python, TensorFlow, Node.js), bringing the same mix of technical rigor and real-world problem-solving to the mission: bringing world-class robotics competition to the community and connecting sponsors with the next generation of robotics and AI talent.

For sponsorship inquiries, partnership opportunities, or general questions, Tony is the primary point of contact and is happy to help.
